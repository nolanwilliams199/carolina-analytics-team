## Common questions

**Why ranges instead of exact prices?**
Two projects that sound the same can be very different underneath. A dashboard built on one clean spreadsheet might take a few days; the same dashboard built on three systems that don't agree with each other can take weeks. The free call lets us look at the specifics and give you a firm number.

**Do you charge hourly or by project?**
Most work is quoted as a fixed price or a not-to-exceed cap, so you know the maximum up front. Small, open-ended tasks and ongoing support are billed hourly at the rate above.

**How does payment work?**
For projects, typically half up front and half on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones.

**What if my budget is smaller than these ranges?**
Tell us anyway. We can often find a smaller first step that delivers most of the value, or point you to an off-the-shelf tool that would work fine.

**Are software or hosting costs included?**
No. Any third-party subscriptions (like Power BI licenses or a cloud database) are paid directly by you, so you keep control of them. We always aim for the lowest-cost option, and many small projects need no new software at all.

*Prices listed are estimates for typical small-business projects and are not a binding offer.*
